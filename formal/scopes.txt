\* THE SCOPE EACH MODULE'S OWN MUTANTS NEED, so that a configuration cannot go
\* green by being too SMALL TO EXPRESS the defect it claims to catch.
\*
\* `floors.txt` asks whether a run shrank. This asks the question one level out,
\* and it is not hypothetical: THREE of the fifty-three mutants measured here are
\* GREEN one element below the shipped scope and RED at it.
\*
\*   BugCmWalkIgnoresChannel  needs a second CTAPHID channel -- the credential
\*                            walk it hijacks has to belong to someone else
\*   BugContIgnoresChannel    needs a second channel for the same reason one
\*                            layer down, at the reassembler
\*   BugMetaAddDropsOnFault   needs a second FID: one to `meta_add`, one whose
\*                            record must survive it
\*
\* Until this file nothing stopped those domains being narrowed to a singleton,
\* and all three rows would have stayed GREEN over a live defect.
\*
\* TWO columns are written by hand: the measured minimum, and THE INVARIANT IT
\* WAS MEASURED AGAINST. The second is not decoration -- a minimum only binds
\* configurations that check that invariant. The fairness row sits in the safety
\* tier and runs one channel deliberately, checking `OpAdvancesIsOneActivity`;
\* holding it to a number measured on `NoAuthorizationBypass` would be a red for
\* the wrong reason. Everything else -- which constants exist, which module owns
\* a configuration, what each one assigns -- is derived by scripts/scope_gate.py,
\* because a hand-kept copy of a derivable fact is the rot this tree has paid for
\* twice already.
\*
\* A minimum EQUAL to the shipped value does not mean bigger is unnecessary. It
\* means nothing in the roster probes above it. Reading it the other way is the
\* same mistake as reading a floor as a proof.
\*
\* Columns: <module>  <scope constant>  <measured minimum, or - >  <invariant, or - >
